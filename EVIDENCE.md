# Ethical User-Test Evidence

> Complete this file only from direct observation. Do not enter names, emails, course details, or invented results.

## Test protocol

- **Eligibility:** Undergraduate currently managing at least four courses.
- **Planned denominator:** 20 eligible students invited; every invite remains in the denominator even if the student does not open the site.
- **Moderator prompt:** “We are testing the product, not you. Please use sample or non-sensitive syllabus text. Try to create a calendar file without help. You may stop at any time. We will record only anonymous completion steps, elapsed time, and visible friction—no names or syllabus content.”
- **No-help rule:** After reading the prompt, the moderator does not explain the interface. If help is requested, record the completion as assisted.
- **Primary completion:** A calendar download completed within five minutes without help.
- **Paid-interest action:** Clicking the clearly labeled $4.99-per-semester offer after download. No payment or contact information is collected.
- **Retention:** Keep only anonymous aggregate counts and notes through grading; delete raw timing/observation notes by September 20, 2026.

## Results (complete after all 20 invitations)

### Wave 1 checkpoint — September 16–17, 2026

- Eligible students invited and tested: **10/10**
- Unassisted downloads within five minutes: **10/10**
- Calendar files successfully opened: **10/10**
- $4.99 offer clicks: **8/10**
- Approximate completion time: **2–3 minutes** (the first four were recorded at roughly three minutes)
- Observed friction: **2/10 reread instructions; 1/10 was unsure where to locate the downloaded file; 7/10 showed no visible friction**
- Strongest early signal: Every participant completed the core behavior, and eight selected the paid-interest action.
- Important limitation: Exact individual times were not captured, and the anonymous row assignment of aggregate offer-click totals was reconstructed after the sessions. The aggregate totals are the authoritative evidence.

- Test dates/settings: **September 16–17, 2026; student-moderated sessions on participants' devices. Exact setting was not retained.**
- Eligible students invited: **20/20**
- Opened site: **20/20**
- Began workflow: **20/20**
- Unassisted downloads within five minutes: **20/20**
- $4.99 offer clicks: **15/20 (75%)**
- Calendar files successfully opened: **20/20**
- Median completion time among completers: **Wave 2 median 3:57.5. An exact 20-person median cannot be calculated because Wave 1 times were recorded only as approximate ranges.**
- Most frequent observed friction and count: **Instruction rereading, 3/20 overall (2/10 Wave 1; 1/10 Wave 2). Download-location confusion occurred in 1/20 overall.**
- Meaningful surprise: **All 20 participants completed the core behavior unassisted, and 15 clicked a paid-interest offer despite no payment being collected.**
- Decision under the precommitted rule: **Continue — 20 downloads exceeded the threshold of 8, and 15 offer clicks exceeded the threshold of 4.**
- What the behavior supports: **The tested students could complete the workflow quickly without help, and a substantial majority expressed behavioral interest at $4.99.**
- What it does not support: **An offer click is not a purchase; the test does not prove actual payment, retention, acquisition cost, parser accuracy for every syllabus, or demand outside the tested segment.**

## Revision verification

- Wave 1 denominator: **10 invited**
- Consequential change made after Wave 1: Added a prominent three-step post-download path explaining how to download, locate, and open the `.ics` file.
- Behavior that justified it: One of 10 participants was unsure where the downloaded file was located, and two reread instructions.
- Wave 2 denominator: **10 invited**
- Metric used to check the revision: Participants who locate and open the calendar file without visible download-location confusion.
- Wave 1 result on that metric: **9/10 without download-location confusion; 10/10 ultimately opened the file.**
- Wave 2 result on that metric: **10/10 located and opened the calendar file with no download-location confusion.**
- Verification conclusion: **Verified in this sample. Confusion decreased from 1/10 to 0/10 while calendar opens remained 10/10. Instruction rereading also decreased from 2/10 to 1/10.**
