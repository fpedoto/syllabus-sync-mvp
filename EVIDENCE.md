# Ethical User-Test Evidence

> Complete this file only from direct observation. Do not enter names, emails, course details, or invented results.

## Test protocol

- **Eligibility:** Undergraduate currently managing at least four courses.
- **Planned denominator:** 20 eligible students invited; every invite remains in the denominator even if the student does not open the site.
- **Moderator prompt:** “We are testing the product, not you. Please use sample or non-sensitive syllabus text. Try to create a calendar file without help. You may stop at any time. We will record only anonymous completion steps, elapsed time, and visible friction—no names or syllabus content.”
- **No-help rule:** After reading the prompt, the moderator does not explain the interface. If help is requested, record the completion as assisted.
- **Primary completion:** A calendar download completed within five minutes without help.
- **Paid-interest action:** Clicking the clearly labeled $4.99-per-semester offer after download. No payment or contact information is collected.
- **Retention:** Keep only anonymous aggregate counts and notes through grading; delete raw timing/observation notes by September 20, 2026.
