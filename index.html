Honest limitations

Automatic extraction can be wrong.

SyllabusSync looks for dated assignments, homework, projects, papers, quizzes, midterms, finals, and exams. It intentionally leaves out dated chapter, topic, lecture, and weekly schedule rows unless they also contain assessed work. Syllabus formats vary, so review every title and date before downloading. Relative dates such as “next Friday” may be missed.

SyllabusSync is an early student MVP. No accounts, uploads, payments, or permanent syllabus storage.
